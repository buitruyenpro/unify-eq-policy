SONIQ Privacy

Request deletion of your data

This page explains how users of SONIQ Equalizer & Bass Boost can request deletion of data they voluntarily provide to the developer.

Send your data deletion request to:

unifyeq@gmail.com
Email deletion request

What you can request to delete

You may ask us to delete data that you voluntarily submitted through SONIQ, including:

  • Feedback messages and other text submitted through the app.
  • An email address you optionally included with feedback.
  • Optional technical diagnostic information attached to a feedback submission.
  • Other information that can reasonably be linked to the feedback record you identify.

How to submit a request

  1. Email unifyeq@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject SONIQ Data Deletion Request.
  3. Describe the data or feedback submission you want deleted.
  4. Include enough information for us to locate the record, such as the email address used in the feedback or an approximate submission date.

Recommended request format

Subject: SONIQ Data Deletion Request Hello, I am requesting deletion of data I submitted through SONIQ Equalizer & Bass Boost. Email used in the feedback (if any): Approximate date of submission: Details that may help identify the feedback: Please confirm when the applicable data has been deleted.

Verification

To protect users from unauthorized deletion requests, we may ask for limited information needed to verify that the requester is associated with the data being requested for deletion. Please do not send passwords, payment details, government IDs, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Data not covered by this request

SONIQ does not require a user account. Audio used by SONIQ for equalization and signal analysis is processed on the device and is not uploaded as an audio file for storage by the developer. Data that is not retained by the developer cannot be deleted from developer storage.

Retention and legal exceptions

After we can reasonably identify the relevant record, we will delete applicable developer-controlled data associated with the request. Some information may be retained when necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, maintain security, or satisfy legitimate record-keeping obligations. Data handled solely by platform or service providers may also be subject to their own retention and deletion processes.

SONIQ does not use this deletion process to delete a Google Play purchase record, Google account data, or other information controlled directly by Google or another platform provider.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests related to SONIQ Equalizer & Bass Boost, contact unifyeq@gmail.com.
